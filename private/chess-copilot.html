How moves are ratedEach move is compared with Stockfish evaluations of the position before and after it, from the mover’s perspective. Labels use evaluation loss: Best (engine’s top move), Excellent (≤0.10 pawns), Good (≤0.30), Inaccuracy (≤0.80), Mistake (≤1.50), and Blunder (>1.50). Brilliant is reserved for a best move that makes a sound, immediately capturable piece sacrifice. Book is not shown because this build has no opening database. Ratings remain pending if the engine cannot complete both position analyses.

Moves are validated by chess.js. Evals and lines come from the engine named in the status pill. The notes are rule-based checks, not engine output.
